Quick answers

Is Mathlo free? Are there ads?

There are no ads and no data selling. [CONFIRM PRICING/PURCHASES]

Do I need an account?

No. You play as a guest. Your progress is saved on your phone. Sign-in is planned for a later update.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your phone. We don't receive it. That also means it can't be recovered if you uninstall the app, clear its data or lose the phone. Moving progress to a new phone isn't available yet.

My reminder didn't show up

Check three things:

  1. Mathlo's reminder switch is on in Settings → Reminders.
  2. Notifications are allowed for Mathlo in your phone's settings.
  3. On Android, battery saver can delay reminders. Set Mathlo to "Unrestricted" or "Not optimised" in battery settings.
My streak was lost

Tell us what happened by email, including the day you noticed it. Changing your phone's date or time zone can affect which day Mathlo thinks it is.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete all my data, or see Delete your data.

Do you sell my data?

No. Read the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Does Mathlo guarantee better exam scores?

No. It's a fun way to practise. See the Terms.
