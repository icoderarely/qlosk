Privacy Policy

Version 2 (optional sign-in and sync) · Effective 28 October 2026

Short version: you can play as a guest and everything stays on your phone. If you choose to sign in with Google, we save your progress to your account so it is safe and can move to a new phone. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

In one minute
  • Signing in is optional. Guests' progress never leaves the phone.
  • If you sign in, your Google account email and name, and your game progress, are stored with Google Firebase (servers in Mumbai, India).
  • No ads. No analytics. No location. No contacts. Nothing sold.
  • Delete your account any time: Settings → Account → Delete account.

1. Who we are

Mathlo is a daily brain-training app. It is run by Qlosk, Mumbai, India ("we", "us"). We decide why and how the data described here is used. Questions about privacy? Write to mathlo@qlosk.com.

2. Guests (no sign-in)

Everything stays on your phone, in the app's private storage, and we don't receive it:

  • Progress: XP, level, streaks and streak freezes, scores, personal bests, round history, levels and stars, practice stats, quests, achievements, mascot outfits.
  • Your display name (starts as "Player") and your learning goal.
  • Settings: sound, haptics, timer, reminders and their times, play length.
  • A short error log (the last 50 errors: time and message), to help fix bugs. It stays on your phone unless you choose to send it to us yourself.

All of it is wiped when you uninstall Mathlo or use Settings → Account → Delete all my data. Your phone's own backup (for example Google backup) may include app data if you turned backups on. That is controlled by your phone, not by us.

4. What we do NOT collect

  • No phone number, no password (Google handles sign-in).
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No advertising ID. No ads at all.
  • No analytics and no crash-reporting service.
  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we don't share it for advertising.

5. Service providers and other parties

  • Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores sign-in and synced data for us. The database is in Google's asia-south1 (Mumbai, India) region. Google may process some data in other countries to run these services. Firebase's privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Google Play. You get the app through Google Play, which has its own privacy policy. If you allow "share usage and diagnostics" on your phone, Google may give us anonymous crash and usage counts through its developer tools. We can't tell who you are from these.
  • Share sheet. If you share a result or a challenge code, the app you pick (WhatsApp, Instagram and so on) receives it and its own policy applies.
  • Review prompt. If the app asks you to rate it, Google shows and handles the prompt.
  • This website uses no cookies, analytics or ads. The host of qlosk.com may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of visit) for a short time for security.

6. Phone permissions

Mathlo asks for one permission you will notice: notifications, for your daily and streak reminders. They are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us. You can say no and still play, and change it any time in your phone's settings. On Android the app also declares permissions that don't show a prompt: internet access (only used for sign-in and sync), restoring reminders after a restart, and vibration.

7. Children and age rules

Accounts are for people aged 18 or older; the app asks you to confirm this before you sign in. If we learn that someone younger has an account, we delete it. Guest mode is open to everyone and sends us no personal data. If you are a parent and think your child has signed in, write to mathlo@qlosk.com and we will delete the account.

8. How long we keep data

  • On your phone: until you uninstall the app, clear its data, or use Delete all my data.
  • Account and synced progress: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your synced progress and your sign-in record straight away (see Delete your account). We don't run separate backups; Google may keep deleted data in its own systems for a limited time before it is fully erased, under its data deletion terms.
  • Emails with us: as long as we need them to help you, then deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

9. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see your data, correct it, erase it, nominate someone to act for you, and have complaints heard. In the European Union or UK, GDPR also gives you the right to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to your data protection authority. For EU/UK users, we rely on your consent when you sign in, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working.

The fastest way to erase your data is Settings → Account → Delete account in the app. To use any other right, or to complain, contact our Grievance Officer: Navneet Anand, dev@qlosk.com. We reply within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can approach the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Security

Synced data travels over encrypted connections and is stored by Google Firebase, which encrypts it at rest. Database rules let each account reach only its own data. On your phone, data sits in the app's private storage, protected by your screen lock and the app sandbox. No system is perfectly safe, so keep your phone and Google account secure.

11. If something goes wrong

If a breach puts your personal data at risk, we will tell the people affected and the authorities as the law requires, without undue delay, and explain what happened and what to do.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the app changes, for example before we add leagues. We'll post the new version here, change the date at the top, and tell you in the app about big changes.

13. Governing law

This policy follows the laws of India.
