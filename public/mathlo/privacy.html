3. What we do NOT collect

  • No name, email address or phone number.
  • No account or password.
  • No location.
  • No contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No advertising ID. No ads at all.
  • No analytics and no crash-reporting service in this version.
  • We do not sell or rent your data. There is no data of yours on our side to sell.

4. Third parties

This version of Mathlo does not send your data to any third-party service. It does not use Firebase, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

  • Apple and Google. You get the app through the App Store or Google Play, and they have their own privacy policies. If you have switched on "share diagnostics with developers" on your phone, they may give us anonymous crash and usage counts through their developer tools. We cannot tell who you are from these.
  • Share sheet. If you share your result, the app you choose (WhatsApp, Instagram and so on) receives the picture and its own policy applies.
  • Store review prompt. If the app asks you to rate it, the prompt is shown and handled by Apple or Google.
  • Our website. The Privacy and Terms links open this website in your browser. These pages use no cookies, no analytics and no ads. Our web host may keep standard server logs (like IP address and time of visit) for security. [CONFIRM HOST + LOG RETENTION]

5. Phone permissions

Mathlo asks for one permission you will notice: notifications. It is only used for your daily and streak reminders. You can say no and still play. You can change it any time in your phone's settings.

On Android the app also declares two small permissions that don't show a prompt: one so reminders come back after you restart the phone, and one for vibration (haptics). Neither shares any data.
