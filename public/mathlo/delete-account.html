Delete your data

Applies to the current version of Mathlo (guest only) · Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Mathlo has no accounts yet.

Everything you do in the app is saved on your phone only. We don't hold a copy, so there is no account to delete on our side. You can still wipe your data in a few taps.

1. Delete in the app

  1. Open Mathlo.
  2. Go to Profile, then tap the gear for Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete all my data.
  4. Confirm. Your XP, streaks, scores, name and settings are erased, and you go back to the welcome screen.

2. Delete without using the app

Uninstalling Mathlo also removes everything it stored on your phone.

  • iPhone: press and hold the Mathlo icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App.
  • Android: open Settings → Apps → Mathlo → Storage → Clear data to wipe it and keep the app, or tap Uninstall to remove the app too.

If you use iCloud or Google backup, an older copy may be inside your phone backup. You can remove it in your phone's backup settings.

3. What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Progress, scores, streaks, name, settings, error log (on your phone)Deleted straight away when you use Delete all my data or uninstall.
RemindersCancelled with the data.
Anything on our serversNone. We don't receive your progress.
Pictures you shared from the appThese are with the app or person you sent them to. We can't delete them. Ask them.
Emails you sent usKept only as long as needed to reply and for our records [CONFIRM RETENTION]. You can ask us to delete them.

4. Ask us by email

If you think we hold something about you, or want an email thread deleted, write to [EMAIL] with the subject "Delete my Mathlo data". Write from the address you used before, and tell us what you want removed. We may ask a question to check it's really you. We will answer within [30] days.
